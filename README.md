# 🚗 APEX AUTO GALLERY – Premium Car Showroom Website

**APEX AUTO GALLERY** is a modern, interactive, multi-brand automotive showroom website designed to provide a premium digital car-buying experience.

Explore 24 production vehicles, discover detailed specifications, compare different models, calculate estimated monthly payments and explore showroom offers through an elegant and user-friendly interface.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Premium Showroom Design:** Modern and visually appealing automotive showroom interface.
- **24 Production Vehicles:** Browse a collection of real-world car models.
- **Vehicle Specifications:** Explore horsepower, torque, engine, transmission, top speed and acceleration.
- **Smart Search:** Search vehicles by manufacturer, model or engine.
- **Advanced Filters:** Filter cars by manufacturer and budget.
- **Sorting Options:** Organize vehicles based on available sorting options.
- **Car Comparison:** Compare up to 3 vehicles side by side.
- **Monthly Payment Calculator:** Estimate monthly vehicle payments using down payment, loan term and APR.
- **Seasonal Offers:** Explore promotional periods and special vehicle offers.
- **AI Showroom Advisor:** Interactive AI assistant for vehicle specifications, offers and showroom-related queries.
- **Engine Sound Experience:** Interactive engine sound and booster sound features.
- **Test Drive Booking:** Request a private showroom appointment or quotation.
- **Responsive Interface:** Designed for a smooth experience across different screen sizes.
